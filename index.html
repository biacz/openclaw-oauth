openclaw

openclaw is a private, self-hosted personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It is not a public service, it does not accept sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's explicit consent it reads and manages the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks so the assistant can answer questions about appointments, mail and to-dos.
